Privacy Policy

Shorts Bot Uploader · Effective 11 November 2026

1. What data is used

When you connect your TikTok account, the tool receives and stores:

  • your TikTok open ID and basic profile information (display name, avatar);
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens needed to upload videos;
  • the video files you choose to upload and related local metadata.

2. Why it is used

The data is used only to authenticate your TikTok account and send the selected video to TikTok as a draft. The tool does not use your data for advertising, profiling or resale.

3. Where it is stored

The application runs locally on the operator's device. Account identifiers, tokens and upload records are stored in a local database. Video files remain on the local disk until you upload or delete them.

4. Sharing

The selected video and the technical information required by TikTok are sent to TikTok's API. TikTok then processes them under TikTok's own privacy policy. The tool does not sell personal data and does not share it with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

5. Retention and deletion

You can disconnect the TikTok account and delete local upload records at any time. Removing the local database or the application folder removes the stored tokens and metadata. Content already sent to TikTok is managed inside TikTok.

6. Security

Tokens are stored locally and are not displayed in the public interface. Access to the device and the application folder should be restricted to the operator.

7. Children

The tool is not directed to children under 13 and is intended for use by the channel operator in accordance with TikTok's minimum age requirements.
